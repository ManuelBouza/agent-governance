# Research Evidence Traceability

Use when consequential evidence must survive the current session and remain distinguishable from policy or acceptance.

## Trigger

Activate when evidence materially informs durable design, policy, compatibility, security, evaluation, launch, or implementation work and needs provenance/freshness/uncertainty/supersession lineage for later reliance.

Do not activate for disposable lookup, immediate-only citations, meeting notes, deterministic current repository facts with no analytical lineage, ordinary coding/testing/Git mechanics, or as a replacement for the decision authority. If version-sensitive upstream behavior is the primary question, use `upstream-version-revalidation` first and preserve its evidence here only when durable lineage is needed.

## Workflow

1. Bound the evidence question.
2. Record material sources/observations and provenance.
3. Separate observation from inference/recommendation.
4. Record observation date/version/baseline where freshness matters.
5. Preserve uncertainty, contradiction, deferral, and supersession explicitly.
6. Persist enough lineage for cold reconstruction and link downstream authority when supplied.
7. Fail closed with an evidence gap when material evidence cannot be sourced or refreshed sufficiently.

## Postcondition

Leave a durable evidence record with question, provenance, freshness, analysis/authority separation, unresolved conflicts, supersession lineage, and downstream reference when available.

## Authority boundary

This Skill cannot create policy, approve recommendations, invent project identifiers/state transitions, declare stale evidence current, rewrite history, launch an Executor, or mutate product scope.

For Agent Governance, Rxxx/Dxxx identities, Research-State/Decision-State transitions, the research ledger, promotion/supersession policy, checkpoint integration, and Human/Orchestrator authority remain in the Maintainer domain.
