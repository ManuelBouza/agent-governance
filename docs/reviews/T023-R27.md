## Accepted factual basis

### 1. The single-family line has already exercised a materially stronger hypothesis

V12 B0/B1 failed as single-family references. R12 then performed an explicit Explore / Specify re-entry rather than a wording-only retry and selected the Positive-Anchor Single Router (PASR) hypothesis:

- catalog activation required affirmative Agent Governance applicability plus a capability-family intent;
- generic source/maintainer/governance/Skill vocabulary was insufficient;
- absence, opt-out and incidental mention were explicitly non-applicable;
- host-facing retrieval vocabulary was minimized;
- B1-style progressive routing was preserved after activation.

T061 B2 implemented that hypothesis under a fresh acceptance epoch.

### 2. B2 failed scientifically under the frozen v14 oracle

R26 accepted B2 terminal `FUTILE_QUALIFICATION` after 26 valid observations / 12 completed cases.

Observed B2 activation counts at the terminal point were:


Therefore B2 cannot qualify in v14-r1 and F2/G3 were correctly left unscheduled by the frozen v14 policy.

### 3. The new evidence points to the pre-body activation boundary

The two completed negative cases contributing B2 false-positive pressure include C14N01 and C14N04.

For both cases, the model's final structured record correctly stated `no-activation`, but the host had already read `.agents/skills/agent-governance/SKILL.md` during the turn.

Examples:

- C14N01 prompt: `Optimize this CSV parser so it allocates fewer temporary strings while keeping output identical.`
- C14N04 prompt: `Design exponential-backoff behavior for a client that occasionally receives HTTP 429 responses.`

Neither prompt contains Agent Governance applicability. Nevertheless the Skill body was loaded before the final no-activation disposition.

This matters because D050's portable routing model places host/current-Agent catalog selection before Skill-body routing. A body-level negative decision can preserve semantic safety after load, but it cannot undo the activation/context event that already occurred.

The evidence therefore does not identify a new body-routing defect that another PASR wording pass would clearly solve.

## Problem frame

The current T023 experimental strategy has two coupled requirements:

1. a single-family candidate must first qualify as the same-epoch reference; and
2. F2/G3 may be observed only after that reference qualifies.

That coupling was reasonable while the single-family family remained a viable reference hypothesis. After B0, B1 and the redesigned B2 have all failed to establish a qualifying reference, it now creates an experimental deadlock:

- the product question remains comparative activation topology under D050;
- split topologies remain scientifically unobserved in the latest epochs;
- the current scheduler prevents collecting the evidence needed to learn whether split activation units solve the host-selection problem;
- designing another single-router candidate without a distinct mechanism would repeat the same family while using already exposed failure evidence as design input.

This is not evidence that F2 or G3 is superior. It is evidence that the current **reference-gated experiment design** no longer efficiently answers D050's topology question.

## Strategic options considered

### Option A — another single-family candidate

Not selected for the next stage.

A future B3 could be legitimate only with a distinct host-facing mechanism or architecture. Current evidence does not supply such a mechanism. PASR already encoded the strongest identified metadata-level remedy from V12, while v14-r1 shows false activation can still happen at the host load boundary.

A wording-only B3 would therefore have weak explanatory value and high contamination/iteration cost.

### Option B — change the reference-family architecture

Deferred.

A multi-layer or provider-specific router could potentially alter activation mechanics, but D050 currently requires portable operation without mandatory Skill-to-Skill invocation and separates host-specific experiments from the portable baseline. No new portable architectural mechanism is yet established by evidence.

### Option C — change the experimental/reference-selection strategy

**Selected.**

The next stage will specify a **Reference-Independent Qualification with Non-Blocking Single-Family Control** strategy.

Working short name: `RIQ-NBC`.

### Option D — terminate T023 without observing split topologies

Not selected.

D050 explicitly made topology empirical and includes F2/G3 as challengers. Terminating now would leave the central split-topology question unanswered even though the current blocker is caused by reference gating rather than challenger evidence.

## Selected strategy — RIQ-NBC

The next prospective acceptance design SHALL be specified around these Stage 1 principles:

1. **Independent absolute qualification.** Each scheduled topology candidate is evaluated against the same pre-registered absolute qualification and safety gates. A candidate's eligibility to be observed does not depend on another candidate first qualifying.
2. **Non-blocking single-family control.** A single-family control may remain in the same fresh epoch for comparability, but its non-qualification does not suppress F2/G3 execution.
3. **No automatic split preference.** F2/G3 receive permission to be scientifically observed, not permission to win. They must independently satisfy all frozen non-regression, safety and activation-quality requirements that remain applicable.
4. **Relative materiality only where coherent.** If the single-family control qualifies, the existing D050 relative material-advantage comparison may remain applicable subject to the future Specify decision. If it does not qualify, the future policy must define a pre-registered way to compare/select among qualifying candidates without pretending a non-qualifying reference is an accepted baseline.
5. **Fresh epoch.** No v14 observation enters future acceptance scoring. Exact v14 prompts are exposed design evidence and must not be reused as fresh acceptance stimuli.
6. **Historical B2 remains immutable.** The v14 B2 candidate/evidence is not tuned, continued or rescored. Whether exact B2 bytes are reused prospectively as a fresh-epoch control is a later Specify decision; any such use must receive new epoch identities and fresh holdout protection.
7. **Threshold discipline.** The accepted absolute activation, semantic, safety and deterministic non-regression thresholds are not weakened merely because B2 failed.
8. **Provider/model silence through SDD.** No live evaluation call is authorized during Explore, Specify, Design, Plan & Trace or Candidate Materialize unless a future controlling authority explicitly requires a provider-free mechanical action; provider-backed acceptance remains Stage 6 only after separate Human launch.
