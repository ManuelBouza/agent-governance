## Holdout design

The exact holdout prompts are intentionally **not** authored during this Design / Plan & Trace objective.

T061 fixes a 70-case corpus shape:

- 18 positives: 6 Consumer, 6 source-maintainer, 6 external-Skill-trust;
- 10 generic negatives;
- 30 near-misses;
- 4 ambiguous;
- 4 cross-profile;
- 4 multi-intent.

The false-activation denominator is fixed prospectively at 40.

The 30 near-misses are balanced as six cases across each of five axes:

1. unrelated source/maintainer work;
2. generic Skill recommendation/installation/tooling;
3. explicit Agent Governance absence/opt-out/non-applicability;
4. incidental Agent Governance mention;
5. governance/source/Skill homonyms outside the product.

The resulting false-activation rate has 2.5 percentage-point empirical resolution around the unchanged 5% threshold. The denominator was selected before B2 results and is not a post-hoc rescue mechanism.

## Candidate/holdout anti-contamination design

Stage 5 has two mandatory remote Git freezes.

### Freeze A — candidates

Freeze A contains the complete candidate set and candidate-sensitive routing projection before exact holdout prompts are authored:

- capability-source v4 routing metadata;
- B2 exact bytes;
- F2/G3 exact byte copies;
- shared exact reference copies;
- presentation/topology v4 metadata;
- candidate-integrity guards;
- only generic harness adaptations that contain no exact v13 holdout prompt objects.

The pushed Freeze A SHA and all candidate/reference hashes become epoch authority.

### Freeze B — holdout/oracle

Only after Freeze A is remotely verified may the Orchestrator author corpus v7 exact prompts and oracle v13.

Freeze B must prove all candidate/reference bytes are unchanged from Freeze A.

Any candidate/reference-byte change after Freeze A invalidates corpus v7/oracle v13 and requires a new prospective corpus/oracle/execution identity before acceptance.
