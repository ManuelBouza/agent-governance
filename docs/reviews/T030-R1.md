```text

Therefore, regenerating the baseline after the final commit changes `source_git_revision` from `43e23a...` to `1322e8fb...` even when the measured tracked content is otherwise unchanged.

1. the committed canonical baseline is not byte-identical to a regeneration performed at the submitted final HEAD; and

This contradicts the objective to freeze a reproducible baseline and AC-CTX-1:
