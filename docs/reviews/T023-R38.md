## Experimental geometry

The accepted v16 design separates development, routing qualification and end-to-end confirmation.

### Development/calibration

`90` non-confirmatory cases are available before Freeze G solely for harness/label validation and genuine score calibration when applicable.

### Routing-confirmatory holdout

`270` unique cases:

```text
60 consumer-lifecycle
60 source-maintainer
60 external-skill-trust
60 NONE
10 ABSTAIN
10 cross-profile
10 multi-intent
```

The first 240 cases form the primary backbone; the final 30 are explicit challenge cases.

### Reliability

A pre-designated subset of `30` routing cases receives one additional independent trial per topology. Those repeated trials measure within-case stability and never increase the independent generalization denominator.

### End-to-end reserve

A disjoint `60`-case reserve is frozen with the holdout but remains unused until routing determines at most two finalists.

This reserve concentrates expensive specialist execution on candidates that have already demonstrated routing viability.

## Statistical interpretation

The familiar `0.95 / 0.05` routing thresholds are retained as **corpus acceptance SLOs**, not automatically as population guarantees.

The design requires one-sided exact 95% binomial intervals for per-capability recall and `NONE` false activation. With 60 independent cases in each primary stratum, zero observed failures is sufficient for the corresponding exact one-sided bound to cross the 95/5 boundary; observed failures correctly widen the interval even when a point SLO still passes.

All common-case candidate comparisons are paired. The pre-registered plan uses paired exact/McNemar-compatible analysis for binary routing correctness, paired case-level bootstrap intervals for nonlinear quality/context measures, and Holm correction over planned confirmatory pairwise claims.

A raw model-authored confidence number is not treated as calibrated probability.

## Materiality and selection

The v15 requirement that a split candidate improve B2 F1 by `+0.03` is not retained as the central comparison.

When B2 routing-qualifies, a split challenger must instead:

- independently pass all absolute routing/safety SLOs;
- be non-inferior on the pre-registered primary routing-quality endpoint with absolute margin `-0.02`;
- demonstrate the retained material context target of at least 15% lower median routing/context load (`<= 0.85 * B2`);
- have no critical safety regression.

This tests the actual D050 value proposition: a finer activation partition should reduce the load/risk path without materially degrading routing correctness.

If B2 does not qualify, F2/G3 remain independently observable and eligible. If remaining candidates are materially unresolved after the planned paired analysis, v16 returns **no topology selected** rather than applying an arbitrary tie-break.

## End-to-end boundary
