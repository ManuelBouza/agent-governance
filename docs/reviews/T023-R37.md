## Accepted research conclusions

The following conclusions are promoted from research into the D078 design boundary:

1. routing, specialist execution, and end-to-end correctness are separate estimands;
2. canonical capability truth is topology-independent;
3. capability truth may contain zero, one, or multiple labels;
4. `NONE / DIRECT` and `ABSTAIN / ASK` are distinct semantic states;
5. routing viability should be established before full specialist execution;
6. repeated trials characterize reliability but do not multiply independent sample size;
7. common-case topology comparisons should use paired analysis and report uncertainty;
8. fixed thresholds must distinguish corpus SLOs from population/inferential claims;
9. explicit pre-routing is optional and adapter-specific unless later promoted to portable architecture;
10. v15's freeze/provenance/holdout-discipline concepts remain valuable and should inform v16.
